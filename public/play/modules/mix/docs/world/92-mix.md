## The mix
The game's sound is mixed, not just played. Music, ambience, voices, sound effects and interface sounds each have their own
volume, and they all pass through one compressor and limiter, so nothing clips and the whole sits at about -16 LUFS.
- **Voices cut through**: while anyone speaks (the Overseer, Pip, an islander) the music drops 8 dB and the ambience 4 dB, quickly,
  and comes back about half a second after the line.
- **Sounds sit in the world**: a character's voice, a blast, a vehicle come from where they are (left or right as you turn, quieter
  and duller far away). Far explosions arrive muffled.
- **Spaces sound like spaces**: open air has a short tail; the Overseer's hangar a big hall; under and beside the pier a wooden echo;
  inside a building a soft room. The blend follows where the player stands.
- The settings (Master, Music, Effects, Voice) each drive their own bus. Effects 0 silences footsteps and blasts, not voices.
