## Progress: points for doing things, levels and what they unlock

The Overseer's worlds keep a light progress for the player. Doing things earns **points** (a placeholder name, "sparks",
until J picks one): the first of each activity (drive, fish, boom, make, dock, go above the air, race, ask, help), every fish,
something broken, each 250 m driven, a race finished, a quest done, a neighbour met, something found. A small pill at the top
left shows the level and a bar. A **level** gives something: an accessory or a color (in the Observatory's wardrobe) or a tool (on
the tool belt, Q switches), and the Overseer says a short line when it comes. It is kept in the Overseer's memory: in the
account when signed in, on the device otherwise. It has nothing to do with credits and nothing in it is sold.

There is nothing to set up in `scene.json`. The earning table and the unlocks are data in `progress-core.js`.
