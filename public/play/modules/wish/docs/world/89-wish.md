## The daily wish: one small wish from the Overseer each day

Every day (UTC) the Overseer makes one small wish of the player: catch three different fish, drive a course in under so many
seconds, blow up barrels without leaving the 4x4, find a quiet place and stand still for a minute. It is the same for everyone
that day (it is chosen from a table of 40 by the date alone). It sits in a small note under the level pill and is spoken once
when the player arrives. Doing it gives points (the level's points: nothing is sold) and a short line from the Overseer, and now
and then a gift for the wardrobe. Days in a row make a streak; a missed day quietly starts it again, with no message.

It is never required and never pushy: nothing waits on it, it has no timer and it is not a quest. A world without what a wish
needs (a hut, a race course, a running clock) is asked for the nearest plain wish instead, so there is always one.
It is kept in the Overseer's memory: in the account when signed in, on the device otherwise.

There is nothing to set up in `scene.json`. The wishes are data in `wish-core.js`.
