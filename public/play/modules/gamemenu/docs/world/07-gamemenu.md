## The game menu

Press **Esc** once (or tap the phone HUD's ☰) to open the pause sheet: Resume, Settings, Journal, Controls, Invite a
friend, Change my look, and Leave to Hawkes Games. Opening and closing glide in about two tenths of a second with a
soft UI sound. The world only freezes when you are a guest alone on the island; with friends, or signed in, life goes
on behind the sheet. Settings keep themselves on this device.
