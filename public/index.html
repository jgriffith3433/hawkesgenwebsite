03 — Multiplayer

Hawkes Net: multiplayer for web games.

The networking every game here runs on, for yours too. Sign in with this account at net.hawkesgames.com, make a project, and your game is online: players, rooms and tickets live on its page.

  • One call: lobby().join('level-1') puts everyone in a level together, and rejoins by itself
  • Our relay: interest by distance and a binary protocol about 5 times smaller than JSON
  • Your projects, live: players online, rooms and who is in them, tickets and usage
  • Web, Babylon and Unity WebGL: one SDK; free to build and test on localhost
  • Already in Sick Of It, Yooba Land and Dose Of Destruction
Open Hawkes Net See how it works
Measured on our load test: 1,000 players on one relay node with 4 processes, none dropped, about 8 KB/s each.
