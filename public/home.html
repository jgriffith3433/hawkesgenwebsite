Free to play, nothing to download. You'll sign in with Google once you've decided what you are, so your island is kept. The box is a taste of the beam: on the island the Overseer really makes what you ask for.
Chapter One

An island, and it's yours.

Pip will show you around. Nobody will tell you what to do with the place. Every quest can be skipped.

Decide what you are

An animal, a robot, a toaster, a shoe. Pick a look and a name at the mirror. The Overseer is delighted either way, and everyone sees you as what you chose.

Drive, fish, blow things up

Take the 4x4 down to the dock, cast off the end of the pier, then meet the fuel barrels. Pip introduces each one by name before it goes.

Ask the sky

Ask the Overseer for anything. It comes down in a beam of light, and you keep it.

Go up

Fly the helicopter until the island is a speck. Then finish Rolo's rocket and keep climbing until you can see the whole planet, and something lit up above it.

Come back tomorrow and the Overseer will have one small wish of you. Levels bring gifts for your wardrobe and new tools for your belt. Nothing on the island is for sale.

The neighbours

Everyone here chose what to be.

And they're a little too pleased about it. They remember what you did and call you by the name you picked. Also, someone lived on this island before you, and nobody remembers who.

Pip

A person, and your guide

Wants to try everything, with company. Has never once checked the wind before a boom.

Admiral Bobbin

A rubber duck, harbourmaster

Commands a harbour with one boat. Very small, very proud. Takes any wave personally.

Mrs Ember

A toaster, runs the post and the weather

Every forecast she gives is right, and she apologises for it each time.

Rolo

A roll-bot, the only fixer

Will not ask for help, ever. Built a rocket and cannot say why.

Dolores

A cactus on the hill

Is not lonely. Remembers every detail of your day anyway.

Channel

A TV robot, the island's reporter

Reports everything as breaking news, naps included. Ratings: record.

Keep an eye out for things left lying around: a logbook with pages missing, planks cut for a bigger hand. Your journal keeps what you find.

Free will

Anything you see, you can change.

There's no editor to open and nothing to learn first. You find out the way you find out anything here: by poking it.

Poke itHold to grab and throw. Double-click anything for its card: what it is, how it moves, its colour, its size, what it does.

Change it where it stands

A boat that's too slow, a hill in the wrong place, a barrel that should be bigger. You change it while you're standing next to it.

Ask for it"A lighthouse." "Rain." "Make me jump higher." The Overseer beams it down: things, sounds, music, new rules.

Say what should exist

New accounts start with free credits for asking. Every ask shows its price first, and one that fails costs nothing.

Keep itYour island is saved as your game. Others can come and play it while you keep changing it.

Make it a game

Start from the island, from scratch, or from one of the Overseer's games. Everything you made stays yours.

Friends

Bring someone.

Invite a friend from the pause menu and send them the link. They pick who they are at the mirror and the Overseer beams them down beside you.

Together

Up to 32 on one island.

See each other, chat, wave, and change the world together while other people play in it.

Moments

Share the good bits.

After a big explosion, a first catch, a long jump or a docking, one tap keeps the last few seconds as a link, with a line from the Overseer.

Visitors

Play theirs.

Every game people make here is a world you can walk into, and a link is all a friend needs to follow you in.
