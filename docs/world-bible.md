# Hawkes Games: world bible

## Areas

- **The Clearing & Mirror** (island centre, ~28 m across, flat grass at y ~4 m): bright afternoon sun, motes in the air, meadow music and birdsong (the inland ambience bed of wind and birds, crossfaded by player x via `H.soundscape`), grass underfoot, looking east down Gravel Road with the Overseer's pearl sphere high ahead in the sky. Players arrive here in the Overseer's beam, step to the mirror on the Overseer's side of the start (panel open soft UI), and leave east down Gravel Road (in the 4x4 or on foot), north-west up to Pip's Hut, north-east up the slope to Helipad Hill, or south-west across the meadow to Rolo's Launch Pad.
- **Pip's Hut & Yard** (north-west of the start around `[-20, 14]`, ~14 m level yard at y ~5.75 m): warm cottage yard with wooden fences, flower patches, a bench and Mrs Ember's mailbox. Quiet birdsong and rustling leaves; connected by a short slope back to the Clearing and a dirt track toward Helipad Hill.
- **Gravel Road & Ridge** (runs ~60 m east from `[2, 0]` over a grassy 7.3 m crest at `x ~34` down to the beach at `[60, 0]`): painted dirt track winding past scattered oaks, pines, boulders and the Ridge Run's lit race gates. Birdsong gives way to ocean surf and gulls as the road crests the ridge and opens onto the bay; packed gravel/stone underfoot on the track, grass beside it; links the Clearing to Pip's Pier and Fuel Beach.
- **Pip's Pier & Harbour** (eastern shore from `x ~57` out to `x ~89` over the sea): sandy foot of the pier with street lamps, `"PIP'S PIER"`, the Catch Board, the Race Board and the Harbour Desk, leading onto a 32 m hand-cut wooden plank pier on posts over clear turquoise water (`y = -1.5 m`). Pier ambience bed of creaks and water under the planks (crossfaded in as x rises past ~60), wood footsteps on the boards; speedboat moored alongside (`[73, 4.4]`), Harbour Run gates offshore, and the fishing rod glowing at the pier's far end beside the lantern (`[86.5, -0.4]`).
- **Fuel Beach** (north of the pier around `[50, -18]`, ~25 m sandy cove): sunlit sand dressed with five red oil barrels chained close enough to set each other off, three wooden crates, a warm beach chair facing inland and a faded barrel drawing. Beach ambience bed of waves and gulls (crossfaded in around x ~28–48) and sand footsteps underfoot; reached along the beach from Pip's Pier or down the northern slope from the Clearing.
- **Helipad Hill** (north-east crest around `[30, 30]`, 18 m flat plateau at y ~6 m above the ridge): open windy lookout over the whole 260 m island and planet horizon, with a diamond-plate helipad marked `H`, a blue helicopter, a scratched tally rock and Dolores watching the sky. Reached by dirt path from `[14, -1]` or by air.
- **Rolo's Launch Pad** (south-west meadow around `[-38, -30]`, ~24 m open ground near the cow herd): quiet coastal meadow where Rolo's tall white rocket stands on a steel launch pad beside a dropped repair slip. Wind and distant surf; reached across the western pasture from the Clearing, and exited straight up through the thinning sky into space.
- **The Overseer's Hangar & Observatory** (high in orbit at `[9700, 4200, -3000]` inside the 1,400 m pearl sphere): vast lit docking bay that catches arriving rockets, with a rear lift rising into a calm round glass-walled Observatory overlooking the turning planet and stars, accompanied by a low synthesized chord and hum. Reached by rocket from the Launch Pad; exited via the lift back to the bay or the Travel Board's beams to featured worlds.

## Landmarks

- **The Clearing & Mirror**: The standing silver frame of **The Mirror** glinting against the Overseer's pearl sphere, soft aurora-face in the clouds, and turning rings in the sky.
- **Pip's Hut & Yard**: **Pip's timber cottage** on the raised terrace above Mrs Ember's red-flagged mailbox.
- **Gravel Road & Ridge**: The **emerald pillars of the Ridge Run start gate** framing the crest of the dirt road.
- **Pip's Pier & Harbour**: The **32 m wooden pier** with twin harbour street lamps at its foot and the **soft golden beacon and shimmer on the fishing rod** at its seaward tip.
- **Fuel Beach**: The **cluster of red hazard-striped fuel barrels** and Channel's glowing TV screen on the sand.
- **Helipad Hill**: The **yellow `H` helipad and blue helicopter** silhouetted on the northern hilltop.
- **Rolo's Launch Pad**: **Rolo's upright white rocket** towering over the south-western meadow, crowned by the Overseer's vertical light beam in Quest 8.
- **The Overseer's Hangar & Observatory**: **The Overseer itself** — the giant ceramic-white sphere with turning light rings and warm amber docking bay visible from every point on the planet, and the **spinning globe pedestal** inside the Observatory.

## Who lives here

- **The Overseer** (the sphere in the sky; a drifting pearl of light inside the Observatory; a soft face of light and aurora in the clouds toward `OVERSEER_AT`): always present; greets the Newly Free in George's calm voice, narrates the Mirror, reacts to creations, blasts, falls and returns, speaks a daily wish, and sends beams from its lowest emitter whenever anything arrives. From the island its sky presence slowly turns its gaze to the player, brightens with its voice, and plays distinct cloud-face reactions (delight at blasts and rockets, interest at a catch, a blink when idle, awe at a first build, a squint at night), each with a voiced line from the reaction decks.
- **Pip** (a cheerful person in amber, at the head of Gravel Road): walks up after the Mirror to welcome the player by chosen name, points east down Gravel Road as the camera flies to the pier, cheers deeds, names roads and barrels before they blow up, and challenges the player's times on the Ridge Run and Harbour Run.
- **Admiral Bobbin** (a small proud rubber duck on Pip's Pier, voiced in Russell): keeps watch over his one-boat "fleet" with a boat horn and duck squeak, rates casts out of ten, blames missing logbook pages on a gull, and remembers fish counts across visits.
- **Mrs Ember** (a motherly toaster by the hut's mailbox, voiced in Sarah): tends the post with a mailbox clank and letter rustle, apologises for accurate weather forecasts, and delivers the sender-less note ("Mind the third plank. It sticks.").
- **Rolo** (a mild roll-bot by the Launch Pad, voiced in River): tinkers with a robot chirp, measures repairs in "wobbles" and "small tuesdays", lets the player pour the last fuel can into the rocket he built without remembering why, and remarks mildly when it flies.
- **Dolores** (a deadpan cactus on Helipad Hill, voiced in Matilda): stands near the helipad with a dry cactus rustle, insists she was not waiting while remembering everything the player did, and waves them onto the helicopter pad.
- **Channel** (a fast-talking TV robot on Fuel Beach, voiced in Daniel): covers explosions and island happenings as breaking news with a TV blip for "record" ratings on his own screen.
- **Barks (all five, 2026-11-11)**: walking past, whoever you come within about 6 m of (and can see) stops, turns head and body to you and says one short, captioned bark in their own recorded voice; the caption sits over their head and in the chat. Each has 8 or 9 barks chosen by what is true: the first meeting, the rod in your hands, the drive just done, the barrels just blown (or a fish just caught), else an ordinary line. Never the same bark within 5 minutes, one bark per islander a minute, one voice at a time. The words never contain a name or what the mirror made you, so each has a recording.
- **Island wildlife**: an eight-strong **sheep herd** grazing the northern meadow (`[8, 30]`), a five-strong **cow herd** on the south-western grass (`[-14, -26]`), **saltwater fish** (tuna, snapper, salmon, swordfish) schooling off the pier, a bobbing **rubber duck** in the harbour (`[77, -4]`), and **shore gulls** calling near the water.

## Ground and finish (canon for builders)

Every tree, rock, bush, crate, barrel, sign, fence, building and parked vehicle on the island sits on the real ground:
the engine finishes them at load from each model's drawn bottom (not a fixed pivot), tips small props to the slope up
to 15°, and sinks large rocks and buildings a few centimetres so no base edge shows air. On a phone, a soft contact
shadow sits under nearby props and characters. Decals (pier deck, helipad `H`, hangar pad markings) use a polygon
offset so nothing flickers. Jaycee's standing rule: nothing floats, sinks, z-fights or is inside out — the
`world-quality` playtest fails a prop more than 5 cm off the ground, any flickering pixel at its ten spots, or a back
face from one of eight camera angles.

## What happens

- **The Clearing & Mirror**: Overseer beam-in score and voiced greeting; stepping into the Mirror (`E` or automatic on first visit) to choose body, colours and name; climbing into the 4x4 (`E`); spawning items or play kits from the spawn wheel (`Q` / `➕`) under an Overseer beam; asking the Overseer in chat to build live on the island; meadow music and birdsong.
- **Pip's Hut & Yard**: talking to Mrs Ember (`E`), reading Lore #3 (*A Letter With No Sender*) at the mailbox with clank and paper rustle, resting on the bench, opening the Journal (`N` / book button).
- **Gravel Road & Ridge**: driving the 4x4 with body roll and drift squeal and a mark by surface (pale dust on the road, a sand plume and fading tracks on the beach, spray in the shallows, grass bits on the meadow, headlights and brake lights from dusk); triggering the Ridge Run (`3` laps through lit gates against Pip's par and your own ghost); sheep wandering nearby.
- **Pip's Pier & Harbour**: arriving in the `Dock` zone; following the **pulsing golden light and rising particle shimmer** along the planks to pick up the fishing rod (clearing the marker with a glint chime and shimmer burst); casting, hooking and reeling fish (`fishing-cast`, `fish-bite`, `fishing-reel`, `fish-catch`); inspecting the Catch Board, Race Board, hand-cut planks (Lore #2), harbour logbook (Lore #1) and lit dock lamp (Lore #4); boarding the speedboat for the 2-lap Harbour Run; waves easing with shore distance and gulls calling every 18–48 s.
- **Fuel Beach**: firing the rocket launcher or throwing grenades to trigger a rolling five-barrel chain explosion with slow-motion hit-stop, embers and shockwave; smashing crates into planks; talking to Channel; finding the warm beach chair (Lore #9) and Pip's faded barrel drawing (Lore #10).
- **Helipad Hill**: greeting Dolores (`E` or walking close) to light the helicopter under an Overseer beam; reading Dolores's tally rock (Lore #6); flying the helicopter up until the air thins and the curved planet horizon appears.
- **Rolo's Launch Pad**: reading Rolo's repair slip (Lore #5); pouring the last fuel can into the rocket (`E`), boarding (`E`) and burning straight through the thinning atmosphere until the sky darkens to stars and the planet stands whole below.
- **The Overseer's Hangar & Observatory**: docking in the hull bay, riding the lift (`E`) to the Observatory, using the Wardrobe and Mirror, and beaming down to Bot Arena, Sky Racer or Leap from the Travel Board.

## Story beats

1. **The Gift of Free Will** (*The Clearing, first arrival*): camera tilts up from the grass to the Overseer sphere in the sky as a presence swell and the wonder leitmotif land, a beam of light brings the player into the world (its hum swelling as you step near), and the Overseer speaks its treated voiced greeting (*"You found your way. Good. I have given you the power of free will. Do with it what you will. Ask, and I will send it down."*).
2. **Who Are You?** (*The Mirror, right after arrival*): camera glides over the player's shoulder to their live reflection while the Overseer voices each question (*What are you? How do you look? What are you called?*); Pip walks over and greets the Newly Free by name.
3. **Pip Shows the Pier** (*From Pip down Gravel Road to Pip's Pier, tour start*): Pip turns east and speaks one short voiced line (*"The rod is at the very end of the pier!"*) while the cinematic camera flies from Pip down Gravel Road, crests the ridge over the bay, sweeps along the wooden pier and holds on the fishing rod as a soft pulsing golden light and particle shimmer bloom around it — staying lit so the player can drive to the beach, spot the glow from shore without reading text, and walk out to claim the rod in a chime and burst of light.
3b. **The Light on the 4x4** (*The Clearing, right after the mirror*): before any word, a soft warm column of light stands on the 4x4 down the road, pulsing, with a shimmer rising off it; the camera turns toward it for two seconds and Pip says one short line (*"Come on — the 4x4 first!"*). The tracker stays silent for the first quest; the light is the instruction, and it goes out when the player sits in the seat.
4. **Gravel Road & The Sea Gives** (*Pip's Pier*): reaching the dock sets off celebratory confetti, a ring of light, the quest chime and a slow push-in of the camera (never the letterbox, so a phone's controls stay clear), Pip's voiced cheer and the Overseer's *"You found your way. Good."*; picking up the glowing rod clears its beacon with a chime and particle burst, and landing a first fish brings a burst of light over the water, the fish-catch sound, the same camera move, Admiral Bobbin's voiced rating, the Overseer's *"The sea gives. Remember that."* and the rocket launcher in hand.
5. **Barrel and Goodbye** (*Fuel Beach*): chaining the five beach fuel barrels triggers slow-motion blast physics, Channel's live breaking-news report, Pip's farewell to Barrel and the Overseer's *"Destruction is a kind of freedom too."*
6. **Ask & The Sender-Less Letter** (*Anywhere on the island*): opening the spawn wheel and asking the Overseer calls down a live hologram and beam from the sky, followed by Mrs Ember's voiced delivery of the unsigned note warning about the third plank.
7. **Up the Hill & Way Up** (*Helipad Hill to 280 m altitude*): walking up to Dolores triggers her dry voiced welcome and an Overseer beam onto the helicopter; climbing past 280 m reveals the island as a speck in a planet-wide ocean as the Overseer speaks *"That is your world. All of it."* and grants the spawn and gravity guns.
8. **Rolo's Rocket & The Lit Bay** (*Rolo's Launch Pad to 1,000 m altitude*): Rolo points out the rocket under a standing Overseer beam; filling its tank (`E`) and burning past 1,000 m reveals the whole curved planet below while the Overseer turns its open hull bay toward the player and flares twice in the sky, closing Chapter One on Pip's *"Huh. That's new."* and the Overseer's *"It is yours, if you come."*
9. **The Hangar Opens** (*The Overseer's hangar, after Rolo's rocket docks*): the back wall's doors grind open with light spilling across the floor and a chord swelling; Pip, Bobbin, Mrs Ember, Rolo, Dolores and Channel are beamed in, walk to the player and each say one voiced line; the Overseer pays off its first words (*"I gave you free will, and told you a shoe would do. It would have. You did better."*); control returns and the player lights the sky themselves (the big button, E or a tap); fireworks, the *Chapter One complete* title, and a five-second tease: a lamp lit on the far shore, *"I did not light that one."* Once per device; replay in the journal (`finale.js`, docs/story.md 5c).

## Music (design, 2026-11-11)
The island's music follows the player (`src/overseer-games/sandbox.js`, "The music"). Themes are community-library assets by name, crossfaded over 2.5 s: **clearing** (`music-clearing.mp3`, calm, warm, a soft piano and plucked strings, the Overseer's beam-in feeling), **road and beach** (`music-road.mp3`, the same tune with a light walking beat and guitar, from x 38), **pier and sea** (`music-pier.mp3`, slower, open, a low drone and bell tones, from x 65), a **driving layer** (`music-drive.mp3`, a pulse that fades in while in a vehicle). Five short stingers, one per tour first, each played once: `stinger-first-drive.mp3`, `stinger-first-catch.mp3`, `stinger-barrel-chain.mp3`, `stinger-helicopter-view.mp3`, `stinger-rocket-launch.mp3`. Budget: under 3 MB in all, a theme asked for only when the player nears its area. Music dips under every Overseer and Pip line (`H.audio.duck`). Until the files are generated and added, the old meadow loop plays and stingers log `stinger-missing`.

## The mix (design, 2026-11-11)
The island is mixed, not only played (`public/play/modules/mix`, docs/world/92-mix.md). Five buses (music, ambience, voice, effects, interface) go through one compressor and a limiter; the aim is about -16 LUFS with nothing clipping. **A voice always cuts through**: any clip of the Overseer, Pip or an islander holds the music 8 dB and the ambience 4 dB down (150 ms in, 600 ms out); the stingers' own dips still use `H.audio.duck`. **Sounds have places**: islanders' voices come from their heads, blasts from where they went off (duller and later the farther away), the beam's whoosh from the beam, gulls from over the water; HRTF on a desktop, plain stereo panning on a phone. **Spaces sound like spaces**: open air is dry with a short tail; the Overseer's hangar is a big hall (2.6 s); under and beside Pip's pier is a wooden echo (1.1 s, bright early reflections); a hut or house is a soft room (0.5 s). The impulse responses are made in code (no files). The Music, Voice and Effects sliders in the game menu each drive their own bus; Effects 0 silences footsteps, blasts and engines but never a voice.

## The Overseer's sound (canon, 2026-11-11)
The player knows the Overseer by ear (`modules/overseer/sound.js`), the way Portal 2 tells you GLaDOS is watching. **Voice:** every Overseer line runs through a treatment into the voice bus — a low octave layer, a subtle chorus, a long cathedral reverb and a gentle high shelf — so it sounds vast and from the sky while staying intelligible (captions stay). On a phone the chain is light: high/low shelf and wet into the mix's one shared convolver. **Leitmotif:** a short signature theme in three moods plays at the start of story lines and ducks the area music under it — **wonder** (intro, gifts, returns), **amused** (reactions from `reactions.js`), **ominous** (warnings, boom, fail). Files: `overseer-motif-{wonder,amused,ominous}.mp3`, `overseer-beam-hum.mp3` (loop), `overseer-sky-shimmer.mp3`, `overseer-presence-swell.mp3` under `public/play/audio/sfx/`. **Beam hum:** a 3D loop on the active beam whose gain rises at least 6 dB from 60 m to 5 m. A shimmer marks a reaction; a presence swell opens the intro. Playtest `overseer-sound`; log `window.__overseerSound`.

## The light (design, 2026-11-11)
The island is held in a golden late afternoon (16.9 h) with a low sun: long soft shadows across the sand, warm bloom on every lantern, beam, glint and the rod's beacon, the sky reflected in the 4x4's paint and the sea's wet sand (`modules/lighting`, `config.lighting` in the sandbox). The Overseer's world has four looks by the hour, each a grade with its own temperature: **dawn** warm gold, **noon** neutral, **dusk** orange-magenta, **night** moonlit blue; the island's own clock only runs the islanders' day and does not change the light (the tour keeps its afternoon). Reference: Sea of Thieves' golden hour on the outpost beach. Graphics Auto / Low / High cut it: High has four shadow cascades and ambient occlusion, a phone two cascades and half-resolution bloom, Low a short shadow reach and no bloom; tone and grade stay in every tier.

**The sea** (`modules/water`, `config.water` in the sandbox: level -1.5 m, waves 0.3): four rolling waves running with the wind, turquoise over the sand and deep blue out past the pier, white surge lines up the beach and foam rings round every pier post, sun glints at the golden hour; the duck, barrels and the fishing bobber ride the real surface. On a phone, two waves and the same foam. The foam and ripple textures are made in code. Reference: Sea of Thieves, waves breaking white against the pier posts.

## Ground materials (canon, 2026-11-12)

Every island surface is a real PBR material up close (`public/play/materials/island`, filed in `hawkes.library.json`): **meadow grass**, **dry sand**, **wet sand** (below the tide), **gravel road** (painted dirt path), **cliff rock** (slope above ~35°, triplanar on desktop only), **weathered pier wood**, and **painted hangar metal**. The terrain splat blends by height (wet sand → dry sand → grass), slope (rock) and the painted road mask, with a large-scale macro tint and distance detail so tiling does not read from 30 m in the air. Footsteps keep the same surface names (`grass`, `dirt`/`road`, `sand`, `stone` from rock, `wood` on the pier). Phone tier loads the 1K WebP set (no cliff triplanar) and keeps new texture bytes under 6 MB. Reference: Uncharted 4, Madagascar coast grass-to-sand-to-wet-sand-to-rock.

**The painted sky** (`modules/overseer/skypaint.js`, `config.skyPaint`, the sandbox has it): four hand-painted 360 panoramas, **dawn** (gold and peach, low sun), **noon** (deep blue, tall cumulus), **dusk** (orange-magenta, clouds lit from below) and **night** (deep blue, Milky Way, stars, a moon), made with Gen `image.panorama` and filed in `hawkes.library.json` (`sky-*`). The dome crossfades them by the hour, never faster than 20 s for a whole sky (a jump in the clock still dissolves), with a slow cloud layer, a sun disc and a moon where the directional light points, twinkling stars at night, and the fog and haze taken from the horizon of the current sky so the sea fades into it with no grey band. The reflections use it. Files `public/play/sky/<name>-4k.webp` (desktop) and `-2k.webp` (phone, under 1.5 MB in all), asked for after the first 45 frames; until they are in, the old gradient dome shows and the painting fades in over 4 s. `window.__skyPaint` is what the playtest reads; scenario `island-look`.

**The first catch** (`modules/water/fishing.js`; reference Red Dead Redemption 2): the line is a verlet rope that bows through the air and goes taut on the strike; the bobber plops with a splash and three rings, rides the real surface (leaning with each wave, drifting a little) and twitches with small rings at a nibble; the strike yanks it under with a splash while the rod bends; the fish fights (the line swings, spray at the surface), then leaps clear with droplets and a glint and is held up by the player (the cheer pose). On the **first catch of all** the camera cuts in front of the player over the water for 2.6 s: letterbox bars (on a phone in portrait, thin bars), no HUD, the whole fish across the screen, the Chapter One stinger landing on it, then control returns. Playtest `fishing-showcase`.

**Vehicles sound alive** (`public/play/modules/vehicles/vehicles-sound.js`, docs/api/50-vehicles.md): the 4x4 has a four-gear engine note that climbs through four recorded loops and drops with a clunk at each shift; its tyres hiss differently on the road, sand, grass and in the shallows. The helicopter's rotor thuds by collective and swoops down in pitch as it passes. Rolo's rocket ignites, roars through the climb (duller with distance) and leaves a long rumble. All sfx-bus sounds, placed in the world; a phone plays 2 engine layers, a desktop 4; the new files total under 2 MB.
