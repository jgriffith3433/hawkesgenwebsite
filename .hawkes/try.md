1. Open /play/sandbox on beta and let the Overseer's intro finish; the mirror opens by itself.
2. Watch your character walk up to the glass and wave, lit warm from the front with a cool edge light.
3. Under "What are you?" pick a shoe or a dog: the character pops with sparkles and the Overseer says its line.
4. Press Next: the camera eases in for "How do you look?" and again for the name; colors and hats change in the reflection at once.
5. On a phone, Ctrl+Shift+M (or any fps counter) should show about 30 fps or better while the mirror is open.
