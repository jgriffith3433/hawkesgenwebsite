1. Open the beta site's /play/sandbox and play for about 3 minutes (drive the 4x4, move around).
2. In Hawkes Studio, open Analytics for product hawkesgen and look at the `perf` events: `ready` at once, `30s` after half a minute, then `2m` every 2 minutes.
3. Each one shows tier, fps, frame ms and, after load, the load stages.
4. To see `low_fps`, play on a slow device or with the browser's CPU throttled; it appears once with what you were doing.
5. Read /legal's "Site and game statistics" line: it now mentions how well a game runs.
